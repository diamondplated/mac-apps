Mac Tools now installs and updates Latent and Sift. This release also carries the DockPeek security fix and the ScreenWren editor fix that were planned for 1.2.2, which was never published.

- **DockPeek security:** DockPeek is now signed with Mac Tools' own certificate and hardened runtime. Before, macOS would hand DockPeek's Accessibility and Screen Recording access to any app that claimed to be DockPeek. **After this update, grant DockPeek Accessibility and Screen Recording once more** (System Settings › Privacy & Security, or the DockPeek menu bar icon). Permission Doctor in Mac Tools shows what is still missing.
- **ScreenWren 0.5.2 on macOS 27:** New captures open fitted to the editor window again. Before, they opened at full size and covered the editor's own toolbar and buttons.
- **Latent 0.3.1 and Sift 2.0.2 join Mac Tools.** Install, update, open, and remove them from the new **Apps** section. If you already have either one, Mac Tools updates that copy in place. Latent needs a Mac with Apple silicon. Neither app has settings in Mac Tools; change them in the app itself. Latent's optional enhancement models are not included; any you have already installed keep working.
- **Clearer progress:** installs and updates show their progress at the top of the window and on the app being changed, and success messages clear on their own.
- **Tidier library:** menu bar utilities and apps are listed separately, apps show their real icons, and anything with an update waiting is marked in the sidebar.
