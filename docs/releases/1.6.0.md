Four apps gain something new. After updating Mac Tools, click Update All in the Library to get ScrollSplit 1.2.0, ScreenWren 0.7.0, Sift 2.2.0 and Latent 0.7.0.

- **ScrollSplit fixes more of the mouse.** Three new switches on its page in Mac Tools, all off until you turn them on: **Smooth scrolling** turns each wheel notch into a short glide; **Side buttons go back and forward** makes buttons 4 and 5 work in apps that ignore them; **Turn off pointer acceleration for mice** makes a mouse move the pointer linearly while the trackpad keeps its normal feel. Acceleration comes back when you turn the switch off or quit ScrollSplit.
- **ScreenWren records.** Record Region captures an area you choose after a 3-2-1 countdown, with no audio of any kind. Trim it, then export as MP4 or GIF at original size, half size, or a width you pick. Recordings stop at five minutes. Nothing is saved until you export.
- **ScreenWren picks colours.** Pick Colour shows the system loupe and copies the hex value of the pixel you click. The last five picks are in a Recent Colours menu until you quit.
- Both ScreenWren commands can be given shortcuts on its page in Mac Tools. Neither has one by default.
- **Sift previews data files in Finder.** Select a CSV, TSV, Parquet, JSON or NDJSON file and press Space to see a table with its row count, columns and types, instead of raw text or nothing. Open Sift once after updating so macOS finds the preview. The preview reads only the file you selected, never uses the network, and gives up after two seconds rather than stall Finder.
- **Latent groups bursts.** A new Bursts choice in the filter bar stacks near-identical shots taken seconds apart and marks the sharpest, or the one with the best faces. Return keeps the suggested frame and rejects the rest; ⇧Return keeps the one you are looking at instead. It only sets pick and reject, never moves anything to the Trash, and ⌘Z undoes it.
- **First versions, so expect tuning:** burst grouping was tuned on test images and may be too eager or too cautious on real photos; smoothed scrolling may travel a little differently from unsmoothed in some apps.
